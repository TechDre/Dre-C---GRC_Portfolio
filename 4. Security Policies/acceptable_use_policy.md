# Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

**Organization:** TechNova Solutions (Fictional)
**Document Owner:** GRC / HR / Legal
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Review Cycle:** Annual
**Version:** 1.2
**Framework Alignment:** NIST SP 800-53 (PL-4), ISO/IEC 27001 (A.5.10, A.6.2)

---

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Acceptable Use Policy is to define the acceptable use of TechNova Solutions' information systems, network infrastructure, devices, and data. This policy protects TechNova, its employees, customers, and partners from risks arising from misuse of organizational resources.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, consultants, temporary workers, and any other individuals who are granted access to TechNova systems, regardless of their physical location or whether they use company-owned or personally owned devices.

---

## 3. General Use and Ownership

- Organizational systems and data are provided for business purposes. Incidental personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with productivity, consume excessive resources, or violate this policy.
- Users should have no expectation of privacy when using TechNova-owned systems or network infrastructure. TechNova reserves the right to monitor, inspect, and audit all activity on organizational systems.
- All data created, stored, transmitted, or processed using TechNova systems remains the property of TechNova Solutions.

---

## 4. Acceptable Use

Employees may use organizational systems and resources to:

- Perform legitimate job functions and business operations
- Communicate with colleagues, customers, and partners in a professional manner
- Access work-related internet resources for business purposes
- Complete approved training and professional development activities

---

## 5. Prohibited Use

The following activities are strictly prohibited on TechNova systems, networks, and devices:

**Security Violations:**
- Attempting to circumvent, disable, or bypass security controls
- Introducing malware, viruses, or malicious code into organizational systems
- Attempting unauthorized access to systems, accounts, or data
- Using TechNova systems to conduct attacks or reconnaissance against external systems

**Data Misuse:**
- Sharing, transmitting, or storing sensitive organizational data in unauthorized locations (e.g., personal cloud storage, personal email)
- Accessing, copying, or sharing data beyond the scope of job responsibilities
- Disclosing confidential organizational information to unauthorized parties

**Inappropriate Content:**
- Accessing, downloading, or distributing pornographic, hateful, discriminatory, or otherwise offensive content
- Harassment, bullying, or threatening communications directed at colleagues, customers, or third parties

**Legal Violations:**
- Violating copyright, intellectual property, or licensing agreements
- Conducting personal commercial activities, solicitation, or unauthorized fundraising on company systems
- Using TechNova systems to engage in any illegal activity

---

## 6. Email and Communications

- Business email accounts are provided for professional communications. Employees should not use personal email accounts for business communications involving sensitive or confidential information.
- Employees must exercise caution when opening email attachments or clicking links from unknown or unexpected senders.
- Confidential information must not be transmitted via unencrypted email unless specifically authorized.

---

## 7. Mobile and Remote Work

- Employees working remotely must use the organization's VPN to access internal systems and data.
- Company data must not be stored on unmanaged personal devices unless approved by IT and encrypted.
- All devices used to access organizational resources must have screen lock enabled and, where applicable, full-disk encryption enforced.

---

## 8. Social Media

- Employees must not represent themselves as speaking on behalf of TechNova on personal social media accounts without prior written authorization.
- Confidential, proprietary, or sensitive information about TechNova must not be shared on social media platforms.

---

## 9. Software and Licensing

- Employees must not install unauthorized software on company devices.
- All software installed on TechNova systems must be properly licensed.
- Requests to install new software must be submitted to IT for approval.

---

## 10. Reporting Violations

Employees who observe, suspect, or are made aware of a potential policy violation or security concern must report it promptly to their manager or the Security Team at security@technovasolutions.example.com.

---

## 11. Enforcement and Disciplinary Action

Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment, termination of contract, and/or referral to law enforcement authorities, depending on the severity and nature of the violation.

---

## 12. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy must be documented and approved in writing by the CISO or designated security authority.

---

## 13. Policy Review

This policy will be reviewed annually or following any significant organizational change, security incident, or regulatory update. The GRC team is responsible for initiating and coordinating the review process. All revisions must be approved by the CISO and communicated to all applicable personnel.

---

*This document is a fictional policy created for portfolio demonstration purposes. TechNova Solutions is a fictional organization.*
