# 🛡️ GRC Risk Register

This repository demonstrates a structured Risk Register designed for organizations establishing or maturing their security program. It showcases the ability to **identify, evaluate, score, prioritize, and document risks** using industry-standard frameworks.

A risk register is a living document — it evolves as the threat landscape, business operations, and control environment change.

---

## 📘 Purpose

This project demonstrates the ability to:

- Perform **risk identification and categorization** across technical, human, and operational domains
- Conduct **likelihood and impact scoring** using NIST SP 800-30 scales
- Prioritize risks using a **risk matrix**
- Document **mitigation strategies and responsible parties**
- Develop a **POA&M-aligned workflow** for tracking remediation
- Communicate risks clearly to **leadership, engineers, and stakeholders**

This repository is part of a larger **GRC + AI Compliance Portfolio**, showcasing applied work in governance, policy writing, compliance, and risk analysis.

## 🧭 How to Use This Risk Register

1. **Identify a Risk** — Determine what could negatively impact business operations, data confidentiality, integrity, or availability.
2. **Assign Likelihood & Impact** — Use NIST SP 800-30 scales (1–5) to ensure consistent analysis.
3. **Calculate Risk Score** — Multiply Likelihood × Impact to determine priority level.
4. **Document Mitigation Strategy** — Include administrative, technical, and operational controls where appropriate.
5. **Track Progress** — Use POA&M fields for remediation tracking, deadlines, and responsible parties.
6. **Report to Leadership** — Provide clear summaries for non-technical stakeholders.
