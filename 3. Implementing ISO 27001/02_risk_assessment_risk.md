# Risk Register

This document lists identified information security risks.
This is  created for learning purposes.

---

## Risk ID: R-001

### Asset
Company email system

### Threat
Phishing emails targeting employees

### Vulnerability
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is not enforced for all user accounts

### Likelihood
4 – Likely  
Phishing emails are common and employees regularly receive them.

### Impact
4 – Major  
If an account is compromised, attackers could access sensitive emails
and potentially other connected systems.

### Risk Score
16 (Likelihood 4 × Impact 4)

### Risk Level
High

### Risk Treatment
Mitigate

### Explanation
The organization will reduce this risk by enforcing multi-factor authentication for all email accounts and providing basic security
awareness training to employees.

### Risk Owner
IT / Security
