# Access Control Policy

## Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to ensure that employee access to the organization's systems and information is restricted to authorized individuals only.

This helps reduce the risk of unauthorized access, data exposure, and security incidents.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees and contractors who access the organization's systems, applications, or data.

---

## Policy Statement

Access to systems and information will be granted based on the job roles and business needs.

The organization will follow the principle of least privilege. This means users are only given the minimal access required to perform their job duties.

## User Access Management

-Each user must have a unique user account
-Shared user accounts are not permitted
-Access must be approved by management or IT before being granted

---

## Authentication Requirements

- Strong passwords are required for all user accounts
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) must be enabled where supported
- Administrative accounts must use stronger authentication controls

---

## Access Reviews

- User access will be reviewed periodically
- Access that is no longer needed will be removed
- Access will be revoked promptly when an employee leaves the organization

---

## Roles and Responsibilities

- **Management** must approve access based on job role.
- **IT / Security** is responsible for provisioning and removing access.
- **End Users** are responsible for protecting their login credentials. 

---

## Policy Review

A review cadence must be established to review the policy periodically and update it as needed.

---
