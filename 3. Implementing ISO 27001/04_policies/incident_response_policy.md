# Incident Response Policy

## Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to ensure that information security
incidents are identified, reported, and handled in a consistent
and in an organized manner.

---

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees and contractors who use or
support organizational systems and information.

---

## What Is an Incident?

An information security incident is any event that may compromise
the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of organizational
information or systems.

Examples include:
- Phishing emails or credential compromise
- Malware infections
- Unauthorized access to systems or sensitive data 
- Accidental data disclosure of the organization's information

---

## Policy Statement

All suspected or confirmed security incidents must be reported
as soon as possible to a security officer/management.

The organization will follow a structured approach to responding
to incidents to minimize impact and prevent recurrence.

---

## Incident Response Steps

The organization will respond to incidents using the following
basic steps:

1. **Identification** – Recognize and report the incident
2. **Containment** – Limit the spread or impact of the incident
3. **Eradication** – Remove the cause of the incident where possible
4. **Recovery** – Restore normal operations
5. **Lessons Learned** – Review the incident and improve controls

---

## Roles and Responsibilities

- **Employees** must report suspected incidents promptly to management and/or the security officer.
- **IT / Security** is responsible for coordinating incident response
- **Management** provides support and decision-making as needed

---

## Reporting Incidents

Incidents should be reported to IT or management as soon as possible
through established communication channels.

---

## Policy Review

This policy will be reviewed periodically and updated as needed.
