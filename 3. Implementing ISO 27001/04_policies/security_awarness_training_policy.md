# Security Awareness and Training Policy

## Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to ensure that employees understand
basic information security responsibilities and are aware of common
security risks such as phishing and social engineering.

---

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees and contractors who access
organizational systems or information.

---

## Policy Statement

The organization will provide basic security awareness training to help
employees recognize and respond appropriately to security threats.

At a minimum, security awareness training will cover:
- Phishing and suspicious emails
- Password protection and account security
- Proper handling of company information
- How to report security incidents or concerns

---

## Training Frequency

- Security awareness training will be provided when employees are onboarded
- Refresher training will be provided at least once per year
- Additional training may be provided after security incidents

---

## Roles and Responsibilities

- **Management** is responsible for supporting security awareness efforts
- **IT / Security** is responsible for providing or coordinating training
- **Employees** are responsible for completing required training and
  following security guidance

---

## Reporting Security Concerns

Employees are encouraged to report:
- Suspicious emails
- Potential security incidents
- Policy violations or concerns

Reports should be made to IT or management as soon as possible.

---

## Policy Review

This policy will be reviewed periodically and updated as needed to ensure
it remains relevant and effective.

---

## ISO Alignment

- ISO/IEC 27001 Annex A: A.6.3 Information Security Awareness
