# Context of the Organization

This document describes the business environment for a fictional company.
It is used to practice understanding how ISO 27001 starts with the organization
before focusing on security controls.

---

## Organization Overview

The organization is a small, fictional SaaS company with approximately 25 employees.

The company provides an online platform that helps small businesses manage
appointments and customer interactions.

---

## Information the Organization Handles

The organization handles the following types of information:
- Customer names and contact information
- Employee records
- Login credentials for internal systems
- Business and operational data

Some of this information is considered sensitive and must be protected.

---

## Key Systems Used

The organization uses:
- Cloud-hosted systems (such as AWS)
- Email and file sharing tools (such as Microsoft 365)
- A customer relationship management (CRM) system

---

## Interested Parties

The following parties have an interest in how information is protected:
- Customers
- Employees
- Management
- Third-party vendors
- Regulators and legal authorities

---

## Business Goals Related to Security

The organization aims to:
- Protect customer and employee information
- Reduce the risk of data breaches
- Maintain trust with customers
- Meet basic security and compliance expectations
