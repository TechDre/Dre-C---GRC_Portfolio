# Risk Assessment Methodology

This document explains how the organization identifies and evaluates
information security risks. It is written at a basic level for learning
and demonstration purposes.

---

## What Is a Risk?

A risk is the possibility that something could happen that negatively
affects the organization’s information, systems, or operations.

Risks are evaluated based on:
- How likely they are to happen
- How much damage they could cause

---

## Likelihood Scale

Likelihood describes how often a risk might occur.

| Score | Description |
|-----|-------------|
| 1 | Very unlikely |
| 2 | Unlikely |
| 3 | Possible |
| 4 | Likely |
| 5 | Very likely |

---

## Impact Scale

Impact describes how serious the effect would be if the risk occurred.

| Score | Description |
|-----|-------------|
| 1 | Minimal impact |
| 2 | Minor impact |
| 3 | Moderate impact |
| 4 | Major impact |
| 5 | Severe impact |

---

## Risk Rating

Risk is calculated by multiplying likelihood and impact.

Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact

---

## Risk Levels

| Score Range | Risk Level |
|-----------|------------|
| 1–6 | Low |
| 7–12 | Medium |
| 13–19 | High |
| 20–25 | Critical |

---

## Risk Treatment Options

Once a risk is identified, one of the following actions is chosen:
- **Mitigate** – reduce the risk using security controls
- **Accept** – acknowledge the risk and take no action
- **Transfer** – shift the risk to a third party
- **Avoid** – stop the activity causing the risk

---

## Review Frequency

Risks are reviewed regularly and whenever significant changes occur.
