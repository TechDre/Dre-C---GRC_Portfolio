## 📌 What Is AI Compliance?

AI compliance refers to the practices organizations use to ensure that AI systems:

- Are used in alignment with applicable laws and regulations  
- Protect personal and sensitive data  
- Operate securely and reliably  
- Reduce bias and unintended outcomes  
- Support transparency and accountability  

AI compliance is not only about regulation—it is also about **risk awareness, ethical use,
and organizational trust**.

---

## 📌 Why AI Compliance Matters

Organizations adopting AI tools face unique and emerging risks, such as:

- Data privacy and misuse
- Lack of transparency in automated decisions
- Bias in training data or outputs
- Over-reliance on AI recommendations
- Unclear ownership and accountability

Basic AI governance and compliance practices help organizations:

- Reduce regulatory and legal risk
- Build trust with customers and users
- Support responsible and ethical AI use
- Align AI systems with business and security goals
- Prepare for evolving AI regulations

This learning portfolio references recognized frameworks conceptually, including:

- ISO/IEC 23894 – AI Risk Management

These frameworks are used for learning alignment, not formal certification or
regulatory claims.
