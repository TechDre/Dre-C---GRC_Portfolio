# AI Acceptable Use Policy

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish acceptable use guidelines for artificial
intelligence (AI) tools used within the organization. This policy is intended to
promote responsible, secure, and ethical use of AI systems while reducing potential
risks related to data privacy, security, bias, and misuse.

This document represents a **learning example** created from a beginner's GRC perspective
and is not intended to serve as legal or production-ready policy.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- Employees
- Contractors
- Interns
- Third parties authorized to use organizational AI tools

This policy covers all AI systems used for business purposes, including:
 chatbots, content generation tools, decision-support systems, and analytics tools.

---

## 3. Acceptable Use

Authorized users may use AI tools to support business activities such as:

- Drafting or summarizing non-sensitive documents
- Providing general customer support assistance
- Supporting research or productivity tasks
- Automating routine, low-risk processes

AI tools must be used in a manner that aligns with organizational values, security
requirements, and applicable laws and regulations.

---

## 4. Prohibited Use

Users must **not** use AI tools to:

- Process or input sensitive personal data without authorization
- Make final decisions related to legal, financial, or employment matters
- Generate or distribute harmful, discriminatory, or misleading content
- Bypass security controls or organizational policies
- Represent AI-generated output as verified or authoritative without review
- Upload confidential or proprietary data into unapproved AI tools

---

## 5. Data Protection & Privacy

- Sensitive or regulated data must not be entered into AI systems unless explicitly
  approved and protected by appropriate safeguards
- Users must follow existing data classification and data handling policies
- AI outputs containing personal or sensitive information must be handled securely

---

## 6. Human Oversight & Accountability

- AI-generated outputs must be reviewed by a human before being relied upon or shared
- AI systems are intended to **support**, not replace, human judgment
- Users remain accountable for decisions made using AI assistance

---

## 7. Transparency & Disclosure

- AI-generated content should be clearly identified when appropriate
- Users should not misrepresent AI-generated output as human-created or fully verified
- Limitations of AI tools should be communicated when relevant

---

## 8. Security Considerations

- Only approved AI tools may be used for organizational purposes
- Users must not attempt to manipulate or misuse AI systems
- Suspicious AI behavior or incidents must be reported immediately according to the incident response
  procedures

---

## 9. Violations

Violations of this policy may result in:

- Revocation of access to AI tools
- Disciplinary action
- Additional training or corrective measures

---

## 10. Review & Updates

This policy should be reviewed periodically to ensure alignment with evolving AI
technologies, risks, and regulatory requirements.

---

## Disclaimer

This policy is for educational purposes and does not constitute legal advice or formal compliance guidance.
