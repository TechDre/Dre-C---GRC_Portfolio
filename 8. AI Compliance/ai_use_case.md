# AI Use Case Description

This document describes a simple AI use case to support learning
and discussion around AI governance, risk, and compliance.

---

## Overview of AI Use

The organization uses a third-party generative AI tool to assist
employees with drafting internal documents, emails, and summaries.

The AI tool is used as a productivity aid and does not make final
decisions on behalf of the organization.

---

## Purpose of the AI Use Case

The purpose of using AI in this scenario is to:
- Improve efficiency when drafting written content
- Support employees with brainstorming and summarization
- Reduce time spent on repetitive writing tasks

Employees review AI-generated outputs before use.

---

## Users of the AI System

The AI tool is used by:
- Business staff
- Operations personnel
- IT and administrative employees

The tool is not used directly by customers.

---

## Data Involved

The AI tool may process:
- General business information
- Non-sensitive internal documents
- Draft content provided by employees

Employees are instructed not to enter:
- Sensitive personal (non-PII) data 
- Confidential customer information
- Login credentials or organizational secrets

---

## Level of Automation

This AI use case is **human-in-the-loop**:
- AI provides suggestions only
- Humans review, edit, and approve all outputs
- AI outputs are not automatically published or acted upon

---

## Limitations and Considerations

The organization recognizes that AI tools:
- May generate incorrect or misleading information
- May reflect bias present in training data
- Should not be treated as authoritative sources

Employees are responsible for verifying the accuracy of AI-generated content.

---

## Compliance and Oversight Notes

This AI use case will be reviewed periodically to ensure:
- Appropriate use by employees
- Alignment with organizational policies
- Risks related to privacy, security, and misuse are considered

This document supports future AI risk assessment and governance activities.
