# Incident Response Plan  
Internal GRC Portfolio Project

## Purpose
The purpose of this Incident Response Plan is to define a structured and repeatable approach for detecting, responding to, and recovering from cybersecurity incidents. This plan helps reduce the impact of security events, supports timely decision-making, and ensures incidents are handled consistently.

This document is part of my internal GRC portfolio and demonstrates practical understanding of incident response planning and coordination.

---

## Scope
This plan applies to all information systems, networks, applications, cloud services, and data environments operated or managed by the organization, including:

- On-premises systems  
- Cloud and SaaS environments  
- Third-party and vendor-managed systems  
- End-user devices  

---

## Incident Definition
A security incident is any event that compromises, or has the potential to compromise, the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of organizational systems or data.

Examples include, but are not limited to:
- Malware or ransomware infections  
- Phishing or social engineering attacks  
- Unauthorized access or data exposure  
- Denial-of-service attacks  
- Loss or theft of devices containing sensitive data  

---

## Incident Response Objectives
The organization’s incident response objectives include:

- Rapid detection and containment of incidents  
- Minimizing business and operational impact  
- Preserving evidence for investigation  
- Ensuring clear communication and accountability  
- Supporting recovery and post-incident improvement  

---

## Incident Response Lifecycle

### **Preparation**
- Establish incident response roles and responsibilities  
- Maintain incident response tools and procedures  
- Provide security awareness training  

---

### **Identification**
- Detect and report potential security incidents  
- Analyze alerts and events to confirm incidents  
- Classify incidents based on severity and impact  

---

### **Containment**
- Isolate affected systems to limit damage  
- Prevent further unauthorized access or spread  
- Apply temporary controls as needed  

---

### **Eradication**
- Remove malware or malicious artifacts  
- Identify the root cause of the incident  
- Apply fixes and corrective actions  

---

### **Recovery**
- Restore systems and data from backups  
- Validate system integrity and functionality  
- Monitor systems for signs of recurrence  

---

### **Lessons Learned**
- Document incident details and response actions  
- Conduct post-incident reviews  
- Update policies, procedures, and controls  

---

## Roles and Responsibilities
- **Incident Response Team** coordinates incident handling activities  
- **System Owners** support investigation and remediation efforts  
- **Management** provides oversight and decision-making support
